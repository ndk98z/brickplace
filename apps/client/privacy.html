Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy says what BrickPlace stores about you, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. BrickPlace is run by {{operatorName}}, {{operatorAddress}}, who is responsible for your data. Contact: {{contactEmail}}.

In short: watching needs no account and sets no cookie. An account keeps your email, nickname and sign-ins. Your moves are linked to your account until 90 days after the season ends, then your account id is replaced by a code. There are no ads and no analytics.

If you only watch

You need no account. The page reads the map from our servers and our CDN. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the usual request details (address, time, browser) to deliver the page and to protect the service from abuse, including rate limits by IP address. No cookie is set. Your browser keeps small notes in its local storage, never sent to us: whether you have seen the welcome screen, and, for each account that signed in on this browser, its nickname in lower case to remember that it has seen the first-time guide. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

We never receive or store your provider password or any provider access token. We do not store your avatar.

Cookies

  • __Host-bp_session: keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days. It holds a signed token, not your data.
  • __Host-bp_oauth: set for at most 10 minutes while you sign in, to make sure the answer from the provider belongs to your sign-in.

Both are needed for sign-in. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

The human check

When you sign in, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person. It runs in your browser from Cloudflare's servers and only on the sign-in screen. A new account cannot be made without it.

What others can see

  • The map, its snapshots and timelapses are public, but they never show who placed what.
  • Your nickname is public if you own a team (the team's page shows its owner). Team names, sizes and the ranking are public.
  • Other players never see your email, your sign-ins or your moves. Our moderators can, to enforce the Terms.

Why we use it

  • to run the game for you: your account, items, cooldown and team;
  • to keep the game fair and safe: moderation, undoing vandalism, stopping abuse and several accounts per person;
  • to contact you about your account, using your email.

Legal bases: we run the game and your account because you asked us to under the Terms (a contract); we moderate, stop abuse and keep backups because keeping the game fair and safe is in our and other players' legitimate interest; and we keep or hand over data when the law requires it. Where the law of Viet Nam asks for your consent, you give it by making an account, and you can withdraw it by deleting the account.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and sign-ins: until you delete the account.
  • Your moves: linked to your account id until 90 days after the season ends. Then the season is archived: each account id is replaced by a code made with a key that is then destroyed, so the archive no longer holds your account id. The archive is pseudonymised, not anonymous: next to each code it can keep the team each move counted for, which in a small team could point to you. So it keeps a team only if at least 10 different players made moves for it during that season; for a smaller team, your moves are archived with no team at all. The archive is kept with no end date, in storage locked against changes and deletion. Your items, the records that stop a move being applied twice, the per-player team counts and your profile's season numbers of that season are deleted when it is archived.
  • Request logs kept by Cloudflare: up to 7 days.
  • Backups: the game's storage can be restored to an earlier point for up to 31 days, so deleted data can remain in backups for that long.

Your rights

  • Download your data: Profile, then Your data, then Download. You get a file with your account, sign-ins, suspensions, the reports you sent and your team. It does not list your moves: they are stored per map, not per player. Your season numbers are on your profile, not in the file.
  • Delete your account: Profile, then Delete account (it needs a sign-in from the last 10 minutes). Your account, sign-ins, nickname and the reports you sent and received are deleted at once, and your team is closed if you own it. The cells you placed stay on the map, linked to no one: the moves keep your old account id until the season is archived, with nothing left that names you. Suspensions are kept without any link to you, and for at least one hour we keep a keyed fingerprint of each sign-in so a new account cannot be made from it straight away.
  • Correct your nickname: change it from your profile.
  • For anything else (access, correction, objection, or if a suspended account cannot sign in to delete itself), write to {{contactEmail}}. You can also complain to the data protection authority of Viet Nam (the Department of Cybersecurity and High-Tech Crime Prevention, Ministry of Public Security) or, if you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, to the data protection authority where you live or work.

Who handles it for us

  • Cloudflare hosts the whole service (servers, databases, file storage, CDN, request logs, Turnstile) and processes the data on our behalf, under Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum. Cloudflare is based in the United States and runs data centres around the world, so your data may be processed outside your country; the addendum includes the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Google, Reddit and Discord handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies, and see that you signed in to BrickPlace.
  • Ko-fi handles donations under its own policy, only if you choose to donate.

We do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools. Fonts and sponsor logos are served from our own site, so viewing the game sends nothing to sponsors or font services.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 16, or under the age at which you can agree to this by yourself where you live, a parent or guardian must agree for you. Watching needs no account. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when what we store changes; the date at the top says when.
