1. The game

BrickPlace is one shared map. Anyone can watch it without an account. Players get one item at a time (a brick, a single brick, a hammer or a shield), hold up to three, and use them anywhere in the open area. The game runs in seasons; when a season ends its map becomes read-only and is kept as a timelapse. The game is free.

We may change the rules, the items, the cooldown or the size of the map, pause the map for maintenance, and end a season or the game. We say so on the site when we can.

2. Your account

  • You sign in with Google, Reddit or Discord. You must be at least 13 years old to make an account. If you are under 16, or under the age at which you can agree to this by yourself where you live, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms for you.
  • Play with one account. Do not use several accounts to get more items, to dodge a suspension, or to make a team look bigger than it is.
  • You choose a nickname. It must not be rude, sexual, hateful, pretend to be someone else, or contain personal information. Moderators can reset a nickname that breaks this rule.
  • You can download your data and delete your account at any time from your profile. See the Privacy policy.

3. What you place on the map

A cell belongs to nobody: anyone can build over it or clear it, within the game's rules. The map, snapshots of it, and each season's timelapse are public. The map never shows who placed what. By placing cells you let us show, archive and share them as part of the map, its images and its timelapses.

5. Moderation

Players can report nicknames. Moderators read reports and may, for a breach of these terms: remove cells or undo moves in an area or a time range, lock an area, reset a nickname, or suspend an account for a time or for good. A suspended account cannot play or sign in, and can still watch. To appeal a decision, write to {{contactEmail}} with your nickname.

6. Teams and the ranking

Teams are for the ranking only. The ranking counts cells your team built that are still standing; each player's daily contribution to a team is capped. We may remove a team, or its cells from the ranking, when it breaks these terms.
