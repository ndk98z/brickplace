# Trademark policy

The code is open; the name is not. "BrickPlace", its logo, wordmark and favicon identify
the official game run by the project maintainer. The open-source licenses do not grant
permission to use them.

## You may

- Say truthfully that your project is "based on BrickPlace" or "a fork of BrickPlace", in
  plain text, without the logo.
- Use the name to refer to the official game, for example in articles or videos.

## You may not

- Run a public instance, app or service called BrickPlace, or with a confusingly similar
  name, domain or logo.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the maintainer endorses your project.

Forks that are more than a personal or development copy must use their own name and
branding. Questions: open an issue or contact the maintainer.

This policy is modelled on the Mozilla and Mastodon trademark policies.
